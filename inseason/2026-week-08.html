Somebody is building. They're in first.

This page's first update was called "Nobody is building", because the window-opening corner, where a team sits when it is worth more later than now, was empty. The contention cycle

Present strength on the horizontal — the Week-8 power score, which reads current roster quality rather than results. Dynasty value on the vertical — KeepTradeCut superflex capital across your fourteen keepers. Teams are supposed to travel the dashed loop clockwise over a career: rebuild in the bottom left, climb the left side as young assets appreciate, contend across the top, then slide down the right as the roster ages. The left-hand climb finally has somebody on it.

The window-opening corner has a tenant. Buckeye Air Raid reads +3.7, the only team in SnS meaningfully better later than now: ninth on present strength, third on dynasty value. The steepest tilts run the other way, Gronkamole and Chips −5.5 and OutForTheSeason −5.1, with The Goonies (−3.4) and Allen or Nothing (−3.1) behind them. Emergency Room's +1.1 is the oddest reading in the league: it has the weakest roster right now, so almost anything reads as better later.

No man's land holds two. LoUdPaXx President and Allen or Nothing sit in the grey box, average now and average later, with Buckeye Air Raid and Dirty Shorts just outside it. LoUdPaXx is the one to watch: 5–2 and third in the table, but it has lost two straight, and its keepers have lost more value since 13 October than every team's but one.
