Set every team's roster value this week against what it will be worth later, and SnS splits cleanly. Five teams read three or more points worse later than now. Gronkamole and Chips −5.2, OutForTheSeason −4.5, Allen or Nothing −3.7, LoUdPaXx President −3.5 and Bo Knows Football −3.0. Only two teams read better later than now, and they are the two chasing LoUdPaXx in the table: aPukalypse Now and Buckeye Air Raid, both +2.4. LoUdPaXx itself sits 0.1 below the window-open line, one decent week of dynasty value from joining G.G.G in the top-right corner.

Almost everyone has picked a side. Only Allen or Nothing and Dirty Shorts sit inside the grey box, average now and average later, with The Goonies and Buckeye Air Raid just outside it. The rest of the league is spread evenly: four teams with the window open, four with it closing, four rebuilding, and nobody on the climb in between.
