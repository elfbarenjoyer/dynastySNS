Biweekly Update · Week 5 SnSAllStars

Three teams and a crowd.

Four weeks in, the board has split three ways. Three teams have pulled clear at the top, two are adrift at the bottom, and the other seven — more than half the league, up from four a fortnight ago — sit in no man's land, average now and average later. The window-opening corner is still empty, but only just: LoUdPaXx President, the last unbeaten team in SnS, gained more dynasty value than anyone for the second edition running and is three tenths of a point from being the first team in it.

Four teams are now worth more later than now, up from one. LoUdPaXx President leads at +1.6, ahead of aPukalypse Now +1.1 and Gronkamole and Chips +1.0; Bo Knows Football's +0.2 is noise. The steep tilts the other way have flattened: a fortnight ago four teams read more than three points worse later than now, and today the steepest is Dirty Shorts at −2.2. LoUdPaXx is the one to watch. Its dynasty reading is 4.7 against a line at 5.0 — one more fortnight like this one and it becomes the first team in the window-opening corner, and the only team in SnS visibly on the way up.

No man's land has nearly doubled. The grey box — the middle half of each axis, average now and average later — held four teams a fortnight ago and holds seven today: The Goonies and Dirty Shorts from the win-now side; Allen or Nothing, OutForTheSeason, The Jackson 10, LoUdPaXx President and Gronkamole and Chips from below. Part of that is the scale: both axes run from the league's worst team to its best, so three teams pulling away squeezes everyone else toward the middle. But the squeeze is real. In keeper capital the gap between third and fourth (7,126) is nearly as wide as the gap between fourth and eleventh (7,911). There are three dynasty rosters in SnS, and then there is a crowd.

The market has backed LoUdPaXx President for the second edition running. +3,661 since 23 September, the largest gain in SnS and more than double the next (OutForTheSeason, +1,712), led by Denzel Boston +812 and Bryce Young +790 — Young was the biggest riser last time too. Across the league the money moved but barely left: 90 keepers fell, 78 rose, and the net change over all 168 is −308.

Dirty Shorts is the fourth-best roster in SnS without a quarterback. Ninth in power a fortnight ago and fourth now — the biggest climb in the league — on RB2, WR5 and TE3, with the quarterback room still at QB11, exactly where the preview put it.
