Who Wants Them

Which teams could actually use each of the top 14 potential free agents. A team is listed when the player is worth more than the worst man they are keeping — best upgrade first.

How this is worked out

Compared on KTC value, except quarterbacks (a team starts at most 2) and tight ends (a team needs at least 1). With 14 keepers against ten starting slots, a keeper list is no longer a lineup, so value decides — except at quarterback, where a team already holding two is choosing between quarterbacks rather than against its worst player, and a third is worth nothing to them. Teams whose worst keeper already outranks the whole pool are not shopping and never appear.
