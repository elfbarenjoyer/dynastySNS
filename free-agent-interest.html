Who would actually sign each of the top 10 potential free agents. A team is listed when the player would displace someone from their top 10 at a slot he can fill, best upgrade first.

How this is worked out

Starting lineup: QB · 2RB · 2WR · TE · W/R · 2W/R/T · Q/W/R/T. Position decides who a player can push out — a quarterback competes for two slots, a receiver for six — so a third quarterback is worth nothing to a team already starting two. Teams whose worst keeper already outranks the whole pool are not shopping and never appear.
