The contention cycle

Present strength on the horizontal — the Week-10 power score, which reads current roster quality rather than results. Dynasty value on the vertical — KeepTradeCut superflex capital across your fourteen keepers. Teams are supposed to travel the dashed loop clockwise over a career: rebuild in the bottom left, climb the left side as young assets appreciate, contend across the top, then slide down the right as the roster ages. The left-hand climb still has exactly one team on it.

The two steepest tilts belong to the two hottest teams. Gronkamole and Chips reads −6.1 and OutForTheSeason −6.0: both are top-four on present strength and below the middle of the league on dynasty value, and they arrive on five and three straight wins. The Goonies (−4.0) are next. At the other end, Buckeye Air Raid reads +4.1, still the only team in the window-opening corner, and aPukalypse Now +2.0.

The league's only builder is in no man's land. With the tenth-strongest roster right now and the third-most dynasty value, Buckeye Air Raid sits inside the grey box, still in the window-opening corner, alongside LoUdPaXx President and Allen or Nothing; Bo Knows Football and Dirty Shorts are just outside. LoUdPaXx is the cautionary tale in there: 5–0 a month ago, it has lost four straight.
