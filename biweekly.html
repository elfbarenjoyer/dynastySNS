Biweekly Update · Week 3 SnSAllStars

Nobody moved. Everybody changed.

Three weeks of football and dynasty value has not budged — eleven of twelve keeper cores are worth exactly what they were on 1 September, and the twelfth moved by twenty-one points. What has moved is the scoreboard, and it has already started separating teams that share a tier on paper. Here is where all twelve sit on the only two axes that matter: how good you are now, and how good you will be.

Nobody is early. Four teams are late. The largest gap in the league in favour of the future belongs to Dirty Shorts, and it is worth all of +0.9 — fifth in keeper capital, ninth in power score. Every meaningful gap runs the other way: Allen or Nothing −4.5, Gronkamole and Chips −4.2, The Jackson 10 −3.3, OutForTheSeason −3.2. This is not a league with a rising power in it. It is a league with four teams spending down.

No man's land. The grey box is the middle third of SnS on both axes — fifth through eighth in present strength and fifth through eighth in dynasty value. Two teams live there: Allen or Nothing and The Jackson 10. It is the worst address in a fourteen-keeper league, because you hold nothing a contender wants to buy and nothing a rebuilder wants to take. Note that the box sits right of centre: the middle of this league is not the middle of the chart, because eleven teams are bunched between 4 and 10 on the present axis and one is at zero.

The preview's positional calls have held almost perfectly. Buckeye Air Raid was called the best superflex QB room in the league and sits QB2. Dirty Shorts was called the worst, with a QB2 worth 2,759, and sits QB11. The Goonies were called the worst tight end room and sit TE11. G.G.G's Trey McBride is TE1 and LoUdPaXx's Brock Bowers is TE2, exactly the two names flagged as position-winning assets. Gronkamole and Chips, the team that kept four running backs and zero rookies, is RB1.

The one that missed: LoUdPaXx President was written up as having the least quarterback capital in SnS — Purdy and Bryce Young — and is QB6. Least valuable is not the same as least productive, which is the whole reason this page needs two axes. Meanwhile Allen or Nothing is QB1 and WR12: the most lopsided roster in the league, and a good part of why it sits in no man's land.
