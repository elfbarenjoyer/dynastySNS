What actually moved. Three weeks repriced all 168 rostered keepers, so the chart above is not a redraw of the same picture. Up: Bryce Young +1,056, Kenneth Walker III +969, Jalen Coker +959, Dalton Kincaid +849. Down: Drake Maye −1,288, Marvin Harrison Jr. −965, Justin Herbert −904, Brock Bowers −652. Herbert's fall is why Dirty Shorts has dropped out of the window-opening quadrant and emptied it; Maye's is why The Goonies gave up a place in dynasty value while holding their power ranking.

Every gap but one runs the wrong way. Only aPukalypse Now has a future reading above its present, and only by +0.2. Everyone else is worth less later than now: Allen or Nothing −4.3, Gronkamole and Chips −3.9, The Jackson 10 −3.8, OutForTheSeason −3.2. A league where nobody's assets are appreciating is a league where the only way to get better is to take it off somebody else.

No man's land. The grey box is the central quarter of the chart — the middle half of each axis — and it straddles the crosshair on purpose. A team just over the line into window closing and a team just under it into rebuilding are in the same trouble, so the zone ignores the quadrant boundaries and asks one question: are you average now and average later? Four teams are, a third of the league. The Jackson 10 and OutForTheSeason arrive from the win-now side, Dirty Shorts and LoUdPaXx President from below, and The Jackson 10 sits precisely on the eastern edge — its present score is exactly 7.50 against a boundary of 7.5. Allen or Nothing is the near miss, outside only because two wins have flattered its present reading.

The preview's positional calls have held almost perfectly. Buckeye Air Raid was called the best superflex QB room in the league and sits QB2.
